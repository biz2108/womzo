# SJB Projects Branding Policy

## Purpose

Womzo is an independent open-source
application created by SJB and published under the
SJB Projects identity.

We welcome community contributions, improvements,
independent forks and redistribution in accordance
with the application's open-source licence.

This policy aims to distinguish official releases
from independently maintained versions.

## Project identity

The project identity includes:

- Womzo
- SJB Projects
- Associated official logos and branding

The software licence does not grant trademark rights
in these names or branding.

## Permitted use

Users are welcome to:

- Retain the original branding when installing and
  using official releases.
- Modify the application for private use.
- Redistribute unmodified official releases.
- Refer to the project in reviews, tutorials,
  documentation and other factual descriptions.
- Accurately acknowledge the origins of a fork.

## Independent forks and modified releases

Developers are encouraged to establish a distinct
name and visual identity for independently
distributed modified versions.

Modified versions must not be misrepresented as
official releases of Womzo.

Use of the project identity must not falsely imply
approval, sponsorship or endorsement by SJB Projects.

## Software freedom

This policy does not restrict the rights granted
under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0.

Nothing in this policy limits lawful descriptive
references, applicable fair use or other rights
provided by law.

Copyright and licence notices must be preserved
as required by the applicable software licences.

## Community

We welcome developers who wish to contribute to
the original project or develop their own ideas
based on its source code.

Our aim is to encourage collaboration while
maintaining a recognisable and trustworthy
identity for official releases.
