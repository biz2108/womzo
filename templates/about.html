About SJB's World Webcams

SJB's World Webcams is an independent project created to bring together interesting webcams from around the world in one simple, easy-to-use viewer.

Explore new places, revisit familiar ones and switch between locations just like changing channels on your television.

Acknowledgements

All webcam streams belong to their respective owners and providers. We are grateful to the individuals and organisations that make these views available to the public.

This project was created by SJB with development assistance from ChatGPT.

Webcam permissions and privacy

Administrators are responsible for ensuring that they have permission to display the cameras they add. Please respect the original providers' copyright, terms of use and embedding restrictions.

Webcam streams are loaded directly from their respective providers, whose privacy policies may apply.
